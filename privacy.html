Privacy Policy / Datenschutzerklärung

This privacy policy informs you about the processing of personal data in accordance with the GDPR.

2. Data Collected and How

Data is collected via publicly visible interactions on TikTok Live streams.

Note: This project itself does not collect technical data such as IP addresses or device identifiers. However, such data may be processed by third-party platforms (e.g. TikTok, GitHub).

4. Legal Basis (GDPR)

  • Art. 6 (1) lit. f GDPR — legitimate interest
  • Art. 6 (1) lit. b GDPR — user interaction

5. Storage, Hosting and Third Parties

GitHub Pages:
This website is hosted by GitHub Inc. Server logs (including IP addresses) may be processed.

Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) lit. f GDPR.
Data transfer to the USA may occur. GitHub uses Standard Contractual Clauses (Art. 46 GDPR).

Firebase (Google):
Data is stored in Google Firebase (Google Ireland Ltd., EU region).

  • Processing based on Art. 28 GDPR (data processing agreement)
  • Possible transfers to third countries protected by Standard Contractual Clauses

TikTok:
This project processes publicly visible interaction data from TikTok Live streams.

TikTok acts as an independent controller. Additional joint responsibility may apply depending on interaction. TikTok's privacy policy applies: TikTok Privacy Policy

6. Data Retention

  • Session data: short-term (seconds/minutes)
  • Game data: stored as long as the user participates in the project
  • Deletion possible upon request

8. No Cookies or Tracking

No cookies, tracking tools, or analytics services are used.

LocalStorage is used only for storing a display name locally in the browser and is not transmitted to any server.

Firebase may use reCAPTCHA for abuse prevention, subject to Google's terms.

9. Game Items — No Real-World Value

Important notice: All Catchmon, in-game coins, and stats are virtual and have no real-world monetary value. They cannot be traded, sold, or exchanged.

Game outcomes are random.

Donations (TikTok gifts) do not increase the maximum achievable result. All users are subject to the same fixed caps. Donations may only enable additional or more efficient interactions within these limits, but do not guarantee success or create an unlimited advantage.

Participation is voluntary and for entertainment purposes only. To the best of the operator’s knowledge, this project does not constitute gambling or a prize competition.

10. Minors

This project is intended primarily for adult users. Interactions are governed by TikTok's platform rules. We do not knowingly process data of children under 16.
