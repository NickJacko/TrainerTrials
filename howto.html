🧠 How to Play Catchmon Catcher

Welcome to the ultimate Catchmon experience! Master the art of catching, evolving, and battling your way to the top!

⚡

Live Spawns

A wild Catchmon spawns every 2 minutes during live streams! Each one has randomly generated stats, level, and rarity. Stay alert and be ready to catch!

❤️

Catch Mechanism

Viewers like the stream to catch Catchmon! Every like acts as a lottery ticket. The more likes you send, the higher your chance to catch that wild Catchmon!

🔥

Fusion Evolution

When you catch two of the same Catchmon, they automatically fuse and evolve to the next stage! Watch your team grow stronger with each evolution.

💪

Stats & Power

Stats of both Catchmon are combined during evolution. There's no 31 cap – evolved Catchmon can become incredibly powerful with combined stats!

🎯

Stage Limit

You can only hold 3 Catchmon per evolution stage. New catches won't be added unless they're strong enough to replace weaker ones or trigger evolution!

✨

Shiny Catchmon

Rare and incredibly powerful! Shiny Catchmon are extremely rare spawns that give massive bonus points. Catching one is a true badge of honor!

🏆

Climb the Rankings

Evolve strategically, collect the strongest team, and dominate the leaderboard! Compete with other trainers to become the ultimate Catchmon Master!

💎

Donation Bonuses

Support the stream with donations to get powerful like multipliers! Higher donations give better catch rates and unlock exclusive visual effects when you catch Catchmon!

🌟 ⚡ 🎮 ✨ 🏆

Every like brings you closer – team up, catch 'em all, and shine at the top!
