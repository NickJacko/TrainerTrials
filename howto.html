🧠 How to Play Catchmon Catcher

  • Live Spawns: A wild Catchmon spawns every 3 minutes. It has random stats, level and rarity.
  • Catch Mechanism: Viewers like the stream – every like acts as a ticket. The more likes you send, the higher your chance to catch the Catchmon!
  • Fusion Evolution: When you catch two of the same Catchmon, they automatically evolve to the next stage.
  • Stats & Power: Stats of both Catchmon are added together. There's no 31 cap – evolved Catchmon can be very strong!
  • Stage Limit: You can only hold 3 Catchmon per evolution stage. New ones won't be added unless they trigger an evolution.
  • Shiny Catchmon: Rare and powerful! Catching one is a badge of honor (and bonus points!).
  • Climb the Rankings: Evolve strategically, collect the strongest team, and dominate the leaderboard.
🌟 Every like brings you closer – team up, catch ‘em all, and shine at the top!
