❤️ Catching
Likes become catch chances
Every like you send counts toward your roll at the end of the spawn timer. Your effective likes are capped at 1,000 — but donation multipliers scale them beyond that cap before the formula applies.
Formula: Effective likes × catch rate per like × (1 + global bonus%). Multiple people can catch the same Catchmon simultaneously.

🔥 Fusion
Two become one — and stronger
Fill both Stage-1 slots in a line and they auto-fuse into Stage 2. Stats keep the best from each parent. Stored Fusion Points add bonus levels (+1 per 10 FP). The chain can go all the way to Stage 3.
Hard cap: Level 100 maximum. Fusion only fires if the result actually improves your team. Nothing wasted — weak catches recycle into Fusion Points.

🎯 Smart Slots
Max 2 per stage — every catch has value
Each evolution line gets 2 slots per stage. If yours are full, the new catch competes with the weakest. Lose the battle? It gets recycled into 1–10 Fusion Points based on its level.
Nothing wasted: 10 FP = +1 bonus level on your next fusion. Cap is 50 FP per line. Weak catches today power your next evolution.
✨ Shiny
1 in 10,000,000 — if you see one, panic
Shiny Catchmon are absurdly rare variant spawns. They grant a permanent +5,000 power bonus and if either parent in a fusion is shiny, the result is shiny too. Catching one makes you arena royalty.
Shiny carries: the shiny property never disappears through fusions. One shiny in a chain = all evolved forms are shiny.
