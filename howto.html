Join the TikTok Live
Open the TrainerTrials TikTok Live. Every few minutes a wild Catchmon spawns on stream — you can see it on the overlay and on the website's live spawn widget.
❤️
Like to Catch
Send likes in the stream. Every like increases your catch chance for the current spawn. The more you like, the higher your odds — but there's no guarantee. Multiple people can catch the same Catchmon at once.
Formula: Your effective likes (capped at 1,000) × donation multiplier × Catchmon difficulty = catch chance. When the spawn timer runs out, everyone's roll happens simultaneously.

Smart Slot System
Your team has a limit of 2 Catchmon per evolution stage per line. When a new catch arrives, it either fills an open slot, replaces a weaker one, or gets recycled into Fusion Points if it's not strong enough.
Nothing is wasted: Recycled Catchmon become Fusion Points (1–10 FP depending on level). These FP boost your next fusion — +1 bonus level per 10 FP stored, capped at 50 FP.
🔥
Fusion Evolution
When two Stage-1 Catchmon of the same evolution line fill their slots, they automatically fuse into the Stage-2 form. This chains up to Stage 3. The result keeps the best stats from both and uses your stored Fusion Points for bonus levels.

Shiny Catchmon
Extremely rare shiny variants can spawn with a 1 in 10,000,000 chance. They grant a massive +5,000 power bonus and carry over through fusions — if either parent is shiny, the result is shiny too.
🏆
Rankings & Profile
Your total team power determines your rank on the Catcher Leaderboard. Individual Catchmon appear in the Catchmon Ranking. Track your Catchdex completion on your profile page and see new catches since your last visit animated when you open it.
