🔥 Fusion Evolution System

When you catch two identical Catchmon of the same evolution stage, they automatically fuse together! Stats combine, power multiplies, and your creature evolves to the next stage. Evolution chains follow specific paths - check your Catchdex!

Evolution Secret: Combined stats exceed natural limits! Example: Flaumi → Flamarox → Flameron. Each stage gets more powerful!

💪 Strategic Team Building

You can only hold 3 Catchmon per evolution stage! This creates intense strategic decisions. New catches won't be added unless they're stronger than your weakest creature of that stage, or trigger evolution by combining with existing ones.

Advanced Strategy: Keep weaker duplicates to trigger evolutions, but stronger single creatures for immediate power. Balance is key!
