❤️ Catching
Likes become catch chances
Every like you send counts toward your roll when the spawn timer ends. Effective likes are capped at 1,000 — donation multipliers scale them before the cap applies. Multiple people can catch the same Catchmon simultaneously.
Formula: Effective likes × catch rate × (1 + global bonus%). Higher level and rarer Catchmon = lower rate per like.

🔥 Fusion
Two become one — and stronger
Fill both Stage-1 slots in an evolution line and they auto-fuse into Stage 2. Stats keep the best from each parent. Stored Fusion Points add bonus levels (+1 per 10 FP, up to 50 stored). Chain continues to Stage 3.
Hard cap: Level 100 maximum. Fusion only fires if the result improves your team. Weak catches recycle into Fusion Points — nothing is wasted.

🎯 Smart Slots
Max 2 per stage — every catch matters
Each evolution line has 2 slots per stage. If yours are full, the new catch competes with the weakest. Lose? It recycles into 1–10 Fusion Points based on its level. Win? The weaker one gets recycled instead.
10 FP = +1 bonus level on your next fusion. Weak catches today power your next evolution tomorrow.
✦ ✦ ✦
✨ Shiny
1 in 10,000,000 — absolute chaos
Shiny Catchmon are absurdly rare variants. They grant a permanent +5,000 power bonus. If either fusion parent is shiny, the result inherits it — shininess never disappears through evolution chains.
Once shiny, always shiny: catching one shiny means all future evolutions of that line stay shiny.
